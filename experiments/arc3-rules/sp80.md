## Core rules

- Grid 16x16 (L1-L3, 4 px per cell) or 20x20 (L4-L6, 3 px per cell with a 2 px border); background colour 12. [L264, L332, L389; sp80.core.c5; source-only]

- Liquid (colour 6) moves one cell per frame in its direction (initially down); into an empty cell it continues, onto other liquid it merges and continues. [L734-741; sp80.core.c7; source-only]
- Liquid hitting a bar (colour 8) spreads sideways along its face and continues past each end, so a falling stream hitting a horizontal bar becomes two falling streams, one just left and one just right of the bar. [L742-749; sp80.core.c8; engine]
- A cup (colour 11, 3x2 U shape, some rotated) is filled (turns 13) only when liquid enters its single open cell and reaches the base between its walls; liquid hitting a cup wall from outside splits like a bar. [L203-212, L750-764; sp80.core.c9; source-only]
- Deflectors (colour 15, 2x2 L shape with one open cell; two mirror variants) turn falling liquid that enters the open cell 90 degrees so it leaves horizontally away from the solid column (one variant to the right, the other to the left); liquid hitting the solid top splits like a bar. [L222-241, L765-789; sp80.core.c10; engine]
- Liquid reaching a wall or floor line (colour 1; the floor in every level, side walls in L5-L6) turns it colour 14 and marks a spill, which fails the pour. [L242-250, L790-794; sp80.core.c11; engine]
- The grid edge outside the wall lines absorbs liquid without a spill (the frame sprite has no tag). [L15-39, L734-794; sp80.core.c12; engine]
- When no liquid is moving, the level is won if every cup is filled and nothing spilled. [L706-726; sp80.core.c13; engine]
- A failed pour blinks, removes all liquid, raises a failure counter and reselects the piece nearest the top-left; pressing ACTION5 with 4 failures already recorded is GAME_OVER. [L709-722, L633-650, L694-698; sp80.core.c14; engine]
