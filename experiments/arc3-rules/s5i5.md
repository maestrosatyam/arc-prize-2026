## Core rules

- Arms (tag 0001qwdmnlybkb): 3-cell-wide coloured bars whose length is a multiple of 3, with a colour-3 cap at the base; the cap side gives the growth direction (cap at bottom: grows up; left: right; top: down; right: left) [L2105-2113, L2115-2139].
- Chains: each arm carries attached arms (level data "Children") and the keys it touches at level start; lengthening, shortening or rotating an arm moves everything attached after it [L2066-2077, L2093-2097, L2140-2141, L2150-2157; s5i5.core.c1; engine E1].
- Sliders (tag 0066ghlkyvdbgg) control every arm whose colour appears in the slider; one slider can drive several arms of the same colour at once, including arms far from the key [L2056-2065, L2232-2243; s5i5.core.c6; engine E6]. Arms cannot be shortened below one segment [L2242; s5i5.core.c1; engine E1].
- Rotate buttons (tag 0089rvqdprjwpz, from L6) turn each arm of the button colour by 90 degrees about its base, with its attachments; if the turned arm has a parent and would point back into it, it is turned twice [L2159-2184, L2199-2218; s5i5.core.c3; engine E3].
- Collision: if after a click any two arms overlap, the whole change is reverted (shown as a 2-frame no-op). Walls are colour-15 arms; colour-1 arms are rigid attachments; neither has a slider or button, so they move only when carried by a parent arm [L2143-2148, L2187-2192, L2219-2222, L2244-2247; s5i5.core.c2; engine E2] [L1712-1713, L2061-2077; s5i5.core.c7; source-only].
- Win: every target ring (tag 0087vvmblxkzdi, colour 13) has a key (tag 0064ocqkuqacti, colour-13 dot) at exactly its position; checked after every click, so all targets must be filled at the same time [L2085-2091, L2248-2249; s5i5.core.c4; engine E4].
- Step budget StepCounter: 50, 150, 200, 100, 150, 150, 200, 200 [L1723-1916]; every click decrements it; at 0 without a win the game is lost [L1942-1946, L2194, L2250-2251; s5i5.core.c5; engine E5]. HUD: bottom row 63, colour 3 remaining, colour 4 used [L1952-1965; s5i5.core.c8; source-only].
- No randomness [whole file; s5i5.core.c9; source-only].
