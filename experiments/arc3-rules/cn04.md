## Core rules

- Board: a 20x20 grid shown at 3x scale with a 2 px border (grid cell gx is drawn at display x 2+3gx .. 4+3gx; a click at display (x,y) maps to grid ((x-2)//3, (y-2)//3)); background colour per level: L1 10, L2 12, L3 12, L4 9, L5 15, L6 9 [L684-773, L832-838; cn04.core.c1; engine E1].
- Pieces are sprites; each has connector pixels of colour 8 or colour 13. Colour 13 is drawn as 8 on screen, so the two kinds look identical [L844-847; cn04.core.c2; source-only].
- One piece is selected at a time; at level start the visible piece nearest the grid origin is selected [L859-862, L866-882; cn04.core.c6; engine E1].
- Matching: a grid cell where exactly two pieces have connector pixels of the same kind (8 with 8, or 13 with 13) is matched; matched connector pixels are drawn colour 3 (green) [L924-966, L973-996; cn04.core.c7; engine E1].
- Trap: the green marking is also drawn where an 8 meets a 13, but such a cell does not count as matched for the win [L942-954, L967-972, L1001-1027; cn04.core.c8; engine E2].
- Win: every connector pixel of every visible piece is matched; checked after each move and ACTION5 (not after clicks); the level advances within the same action [L1001-1027, L1076-1078, L1105-1107, L1029-1033; cn04.core.c9; engine E1].
- Overlap of non-connector pixels between pieces is allowed; pieces can sit on top of each other [L1086-1103 (no collision test); cn04.core.c10; engine E4].
- Step limit: MaxSteps per level (75, 100, 125, 125, 150, 200); every action including clicks counts; the action whose count reaches MaxSteps ends the game (GAME_OVER) before it takes effect [L687, L701, L715, L730, L749, L773, L1035-1039; cn04.core.c11; engine E3].
- HUD: row 0, a 32 px bar at x 16-47; its black part grows from the left with the actions used [L794-808; cn04.core.c12; engine E3].
- Grey masking (L3-L6): unselected pieces are drawn all grey (colour 4) except their matched connectors (green); only the selected piece shows its colours and its connector dots [L839, L848-849, L873-875, L997-999; cn04.core.c13; source-only].
- No randomness: no random module; layouts fixed in the Level list [L1-13, L677-776; cn04.core.c14; source-only].
