- Blocks (colours 8, 9, 12, 14) on the arm travel with it; a block in front of a moving segment is pushed one cell, in chains [L871-922; sk48.core.c2; engine].
- Pick-up: an extending tip that meets a block which cannot be pushed (edge, wall, stuck chain) slides under it and the block is now on the arm; a retracting arm slides out from under a block that cannot move back [L881-900; sk48.core.c3; engine].
