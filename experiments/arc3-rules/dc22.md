## Core rules

- Board: void colour 4; right panel colour 5 holds the buttons [L9833, L9608, L9631]. The player can only stand where its top-left pixel lies on an opaque pixel of a walkable (intangible) sprite: platforms (colour 2), open bridges, bars, sliders, carried pieces. A move whose target is not walkable, or that collides with a solid (tangible) sprite, leaves the player in place [L10384-10399, L10840-10848] [L10384-10399, L10840-10848; dc22.core.c2; engine E7].
- Goal: a 2x2 colour-11 square on a platform. The level is cleared when the player's position equals the goal's position exactly [L10859-10862, L10869-10870] [L10859-10862, L10869-10870; dc22.core.c3; engine E1-E6].
- Toggle objects have numbered variants; a button click advances every object with the button's letter to its next variant (k -> k mod n + 1). Variants tagged walkable become intangible, the others solid [L10016-10045, L10401-10410, L10673-10686] [L10016-10045, L10401-10410, L10673-10686; dc22.core.c4; engine E1]. Kinds:
  - bridge (4x4 etc.): open (solid colour, walkable) <-> closed (checkerboard, blocks). One button flips all bridges of its colour at once, so opening some closes others [L10016-10031] [L10016-10031, L10673-10686; dc22.core.c5; engine E1, E7].
  - rotating bar (20x20 cross, L1-L3, L6 "brixto"): horizontal arm <-> vertical arm through a fixed pivot square that belongs to both [L10016-10045; dc22.core.c6; engine E1, E2].
  - drawbridge (L4, L5; 16x2, colour 1/12): 8-state cycle empty -> growing from both ends -> full (12) -> shrinking -> empty [L10016-10045, drfztmbrixto sprites; dc22.core.c7; engine E4].
  - slider (L4-L6; 16x2 track): a 6-px platform that moves 2 px right per click over 6 states, then jumps back to the left end [L10016-10045, moxubw sprites; dc22.core.c8; engine E4].
  - teleporter pads (2x2, colour 6/7): if the player stands exactly on a pad when its button is clicked, it is moved to the partner pad (the other variant number of the same pad colour) [L10664-10672, L10454-10474] [L10454-10474, L10664-10672; dc22.core.c9; engine E3].
- Buttons are coloured like the objects they control. Some buttons start hidden and are enabled by walking over a matching key (2-row glyph in the button's colour, tag piyqze); the key is consumed [L10849-10857] [L10849-10857; dc22.core.c10; engine E2, E8].
- Fall: if after a click the player is no longer on a walkable pixel, a fall animation plays, 20 budget units are lost and the board reverts to the state before the click [L10476-10493, L10530-10540, L10547-10572, L10811-10816, L9890-9892] [L10476-10493, L10530-10572, L10811-10816; dc22.core.c11; engine E9].
- Budget (StepCounter): 128, 192, 192, 192, 512, 1024 [L9622, L9647, L9677, L9709, L9752, L9830]. Cost: 1 per move attempt (blocked moves too), 2 per button click, 1 per other click or crane move, 20 per fall. At 0: GAME_OVER [L10817-10841, L10863-10866] [L9622-9830, L9901-9910, L10817-10841, L10863-10866; dc22.core.c12; engine E10]. HUD: bottom row y=63, colour 0 remaining, colour 3 used [L9901-9910].
