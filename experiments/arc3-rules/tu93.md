## Core rules

- Board: maze of 3x3 nodes (colour 0) joined by 3x3 corridor blocks (colour 2) on a 6-px lattice, background colour 5; level grids of 39-51 px are drawn centred in the 64x64 frame [L49-90, L884-895; tu93.core.c1; engine].
- Player: 3x3 colour 9 with a colour-4 pixel on its facing side [L413-423]. Exit: 3x3 colour 14 [L402-412]. Win: after a move and all enemy responses, every player sprite stands on an exit [L1236-1242; tu93.core.c4; engine].
- Turn order per successful move: the player slides to the next node; an enemy on that node dies, whatever its facing [L1031-1036, L1042-1066; tu93.core.c5; engine]; then enemies respond and an enemy ending on the player's node kills the player [L1076-1101, L1137-1147]; then win/lose checks [L1236-1249].
- Sentry (colour 8, colour-15 nose): static; if the player ends a move on the node it faces, it lunges one node and kills the player; it can be killed by stepping onto it from any other side [L1068-1086, L1025-1027; tu93.core.c6; engine].
- Patroller (colour 12): moves one node every turn in its facing direction, reverses when the next corridor is missing; kills the player by landing on it, dies if the player lands on it [L1088-1101, L1103-1129; tu93.core.c7; engine].
- Follower (colour 13): static until the player ends a move two nodes in front of it; from the next turn it moves one node per turn, first two nodes straight, then repeating the player's moves two behind (it walks the player's trail); it kills the player by landing on it, so the player must not step back onto its own trail or meet it head-on [L1149-1160, L1131-1147, L1186-1188; tu93.core.c8; engine].
- Enemies act only on turns where the player actually moved; an enemy's nose turns colour 11 once activated [L1172-1250, L1019-1030; tu93.core.c9; source-only].
- Step counter per level 50, 50, 35, 20, 50, 60, 30, 50, 50; each press decrements it; HUD row 63 colour 6 (remaining) over colour 0; at 0 the game is over unless that move won; also lost when no player sprite remains [L745-882, L941-963, L1243-1249; tu93.core.c3; engine].
- No randomness [L966-1250; tu93.core.c10; source-only].
