- Clicking a breakable block removes it; if it was the cell the player rests on, the player falls through [L4209-4237, L4272-4282; bp35.core.c3; engine].
- Win: entering the gem cell sideways or a fall ending on the gem [L4074-4077, L4200-4201, L4168, L4234-4235; bp35.core.c4; engine].
- Lose: a fall ending on a spike [L4202-4203, L4100-4130, L4168, L4236-4237; bp35.core.c5; engine].
- Lose (L1-L3): a hazard band rises one row on every even-numbered action that is a walk without a fall or a bump; GAME_OVER if it is on the player's row at that moment [L4030-4053, L4081, L4090-4091, L4176, L4186-4187; bp35.core.c6; engine].
- Action limit: GAME_OVER when the action counter reaches 64 (L1-L6) or 128 (L7-L9); every action counts, RESET zeroes it [L4407-4438, L4498-4533; bp35.core.c7; engine]. - Clicking a colour-12 solid block makes it a passable outline and vice versa; the player falls if the cell it rests on opens [L4318-4362, L4078; bp35.core.c9; engine].
- Clicking a colour-8 block removes it and flips gravity; the player then falls the other way [L4363-4377, L4209-4237; bp35.core.c10; engine].
- Clicking a colour-15 spawner removes it and puts new spawners in its empty 4-neighbour cells [L4283-4317; bp35.core.c11; engine].
- No randomness [bp35.core.c13; source-only].
