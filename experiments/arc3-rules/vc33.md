## Core rules

- Gravity: each level has a gravity vector of size 2 or 3 along one axis: L1 (+2,0), L2 (-2,0), L3 (0,+2), L4 (0,+3), L5 (+3,0), L6 (-3,0), L7 (0,-2). Fluid columns (colour 0) rest against the side gravity points to; their free surface faces the other way; balls rest on (or, with gravity up, hang from) that surface. Walls and stop bars are colour 5; background colour 3, padding colour 4 [L1546, L1567, L1600, L1629, L1659, L1682, L1718, L1722-1723, L1836-1869, L1881; vc33.core.c1; source-only].
- Buttons (colour 9, 2x2 or 3x3) sit in pairs on both sides of a wall. At level start each is bound to the column on its own side (receiver) and the column across the wall (source). A click moves |gravity| cells of fluid from source to receiver: the receiver's surface advances by that amount, the source's recedes, and a ball resting on either surface moves with it [L1877-1918, L2013-2035, L2037-2039; vc33.core.c2; engine E17].
- A transfer does nothing (the click still costs 1) when the source is empty, or the receiver's surface has reached its limit: the far end of the walls bordering it, or a stop bar in its lane (less 4, or 6 when gravity is (-3,0), if a ball rides on it) [L1982-2011, L2013-2014; vc33.core.c3; engine E18 for the empty-source case; stop-bar limit source-only].
- Gates (colour-1 bars, 3x12 or 2x8, set in walls): a gate is open when the columns on both sides of it have their surfaces exactly at the gate's end on the gravity side; open gates are drawn in colour 12 with an arrow marker, closed ones in colour 1 [L1940-1975]. Clicking an open gate animates the balls of the two columns across: each ball ends centred on the other column's surface, so the balls swap columns. Clicking a closed gate does nothing [L2041-2081, L2096-2102; vc33.core.c4; engine E19; the 1->12 colour change is visible in the E13 trace].
- Win, checked after every click and after a gate animation: every ball rests on a column that borders the wall carrying a mark (2x3 or 3x6, colour 11, 14 or 15) of the ball's base colour, and the ball and the mark have the same coordinate along the gravity axis [L1920-1938, L2078-2084, L2103-2104; vc33.core.c5; engine E20].
- Budget per level (StepCounter): 50, 50, 75, 50, 200, 50, 200. Every click uses 1, including clicks on empty space or on closed gates; when it reaches 0 without a win the game is over [L1545, L1566, L1599, L1628, L1658, L1681, L1717, L1782-1786, L1827-1831, L2086, L2105-2106; vc33.core.c6, vc33.core.c7; engine E21, E22].
- HUD: display row 0 shows the remaining budget fraction in colour 7 from the left, the used part in colour 4 [L1792-1805; vc33.core.c8; source-only; consistent with the 7->4 cell per click seen in every trace].
- No randomness; only ACTION6 [L1820; vc33.core.c9; source-only].

## Levels

### L1
- Layout: 32x32 grid, gravity right (+2). Upper tank (x16-31, y0-13) and lower tank (x26-31, y16-31) separated by a horizontal wall at y=14-15 carrying a colour-11 mark at x=17; the colour-11 ball rests on the lower tank's left surface at x=23; one button per tank at the right edge; budget 50 [L1532-1548].
- New: everything.
- KEY INSIGHT: the lower tank's button feeds it from the upper tank, pushing its surface and the ball left 2 cells per click; three clicks bring the ball level with the mark on the wall above [vc33.L1.k1; engine E1].
- Win: lower-tank button x3 (6:63:35 x3) [vc33.L1.k2; engine E1]; the human sequence (7 clicks, 2 off-target, 1 the other way) also wins [engine E2].

### L2
- Layout: 32x32, gravity left (-2). Three stacked tanks with buttons at the left edge (two per wall); the colour-14 ball rests on the bottom tank's right surface at x=4; the colour-14 mark is on the wall between the middle and bottom tanks at x=14 (5 transfers away); the middle tank holds 3 transfers of fluid; budget 50 [L1549-1569].
- New: three tanks; fluid has to be relayed.
- KEY INSIGHT: fluid only moves between neighbouring tanks and the middle tank cannot supply 5 transfers, so it must first be refilled from the top tank before the bottom tank is raised 5 times [vc33.L2.k1; engine E3].
- Win: middle-tank button x2 (6:3:27), bottom-tank button x5 (6:3:47) [vc33.L2.k2; engine E3, human E4].
- Raising the bottom tank 5 times without the refill fails (it stops after 3) [vc33.L2.k3; engine E5].

### L3
- Layout: 52x52, gravity down (+2). Five columns c1-c5 (left to right) separated by four walls (the one between c2 and c3 is short); a button pair at the bottom of every wall; three balls: 14 on c1, 15 on c4, 11 on c5 (the tall right column); marks 14 (wall c1/c2), 15 (wall c3/c4), 11 (wall c4/c5) at different heights; budget 75 [L1570-1602].
- New: several balls whose heights are coupled through shared fluid.
- KEY INSIGHT: fluid is conserved and only moves between neighbours, so the balls' heights are coupled: draining c5 by 9 steps lowers ball 11 to its mark and feeds c4; c4 must pass 3 of those steps on to c3 so that ball 15 ends 6 steps up at its mark; c1 must gain 6 steps (ball 14) through c2 and c3, which are too small to pass the fluid in one go, so the relay clicks have to alternate [vc33.L3.k1; engine E6].
- Win: 23 clicks (buttons named by the column they raise): c1, c2, c1, c2, c1, c3, c2, c1, c3, c2, c1, c3, c2, c1, then c4 x9 [vc33.L3.k2; engine E6, human E7].

### L4

Five columns; colour-1 gates in the first wall (x12-14) and the second wall (x27-29); the second wall has no buttons; the large colour-11 ball rests on c1 at the far left; the colour-11 mark is on the third wall (x42-44), between c3 and c4; budget 50 [L1603-1631].
- New: gates.
- KEY INSIGHT: the ball has to be ferried right through both gates (c1 to c2 to c3), each gate opening only when both neighbouring surfaces are flush with its lower end, and then c3 raised to the mark with fluid relayed from c5 through c4 [vc33.L4.k1; engine E8].
- Win: 21 clicks: c2 button x2, gate 1, c2 button x3, c3 button x2, c4 button, c3 button, gate 2, then c4 and c3 buttons alternately x5 [vc33.L4.k2; engine E8, human E9].

### L5
- Layout: 64x64, gravity right (+3). Four stacked tanks T1 (top, wide) to T4 (bottom), T2 small; walls between them each carry a gate (G1 T1/T2, G2 T2/T3, G3 T3/T4); buttons in pairs at the right edge; colour-14 ball on T1, colour-11 ball on T4; the colour-11 mark is on the T1/T2 wall and the colour-14 mark on the T3/T4 wall, so the balls must exchange ends; budget 200 [L1632-1661].
- New: two balls travelling in opposite directions through a chain of gates.
- KEY INSIGHT: each gate swaps the balls of its two tanks, so the two balls can pass each other in the stack; the tanks must be levelled flush with each gate in turn (relaying fluid through the small T2) and finally set so each ball is level with its mark (ball 11 ends on T1, ball 14 on T4) [vc33.L5.k1; engine E10].
- Win: 44 clicks: ball 11 goes up through G3 and G2; G1 swaps it with ball 14 (ball 11 to T1, ball 14 to T2); ball 14 goes down through G2 and G3 to T4; final clicks set T1 and T4 [vc33.L5.k2; engine E10]. The human session (88 clicks) uses another gate order, G3, G1, G2, G1, G3 (the balls meet and swap at G2) [engine E11].

### L6

A thin top-left column A holding the colour-11 ball, a top-right column B, and a large bottom column C under a horizontal wall (y30-32) that carries gate 1 (A/C), gate 2 (B/C) and the colour-11 mark at x=48 (under B); a colour-5 bar at x=21 stops A from extending into B; budget 50 [L1662-1684].

### L7
- Layout: 48x48, gravity up (-2): fluid hangs from the top and balls hang below the surfaces. Two full-height walls (x14-15, x32-33) make three lanes; the outer lanes are split into upper and lower columns by stop bars at y=22; the middle column spans the full height. Three gates, all joining the middle column to an outer column (upper right at y=8, lower right and lower left at y=30); balls 15 (upper right), 11 (lower left), 14 (lower right); marks 14 (left wall, y=10), 11 (right wall, y=18), 15 (left wall, y=42); budget 200 [L1685-1721].
- New: a hub column; three balls to permute.
- KEY INSIGHT: every gate joins the middle column to an outer column, so the middle column is the only route between columns and between halves; the balls must be permuted through it one at a time (ball 11 to the upper right, ball 15 to the lower left, ball 14 into the middle itself), re-levelling the middle column to each gate's height in between, and finally each column set to its ball's mark height [vc33.L7.k1; engine E15].
- Win: 49 clicks: fill the middle column to y=30 and take ball 11 in through the lower-left gate; shorten it to y=8 and swap ball 11 out to the upper right while ball 15 comes in; refill to y=30, drop ball 15 into the lower left and take ball 14 in from the lower right; then set the upper right to y=18, the lower left to y=42 and the middle to y=10 [vc33.L7.k2; engine E15]. The human session (109 clicks) cycles the balls through the middle with more swaps and ends in the same final arrangement [engine E16].
