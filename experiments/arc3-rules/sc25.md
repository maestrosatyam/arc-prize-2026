## Core rules

- The player is a 2x2 sprite (colours 9/10) drawn at scale 2 (4x4, big) at level start; it can be made small (2x2). [L1381, L2097-2100; sc25.core.c5; source-only]
- The exit is a box with a colour-9 frame and colour-10 inside; moving into it slides the player in and completes the level. [L2734-2750, L1814-1835; sc25.core.c6; engine]
- Walls are the colour-5 map; walkable floor is colour 2. [L1621, duvwsv-* sprites; sc25.core.c7; source-only]
- Spells are cast by drawing a pattern on the 3x3 grid: when the lit cells equal a spell pattern exactly and that spell is allowed in the level it casts at once and the grid clears. [L1650-1666, L1925-1933, L2409-2416, L2231-2245; sc25.core.c8; engine]

- Resize (diamond: top, left, right, bottom cells; colour 15) toggles big/small; growing needs a free 4x4 area (the player may shift up/left by up to 2 px); without room the player flashes and stays small; the cast still costs. [L2089-2131, L2474-2510; sc25.core.c10; engine]
