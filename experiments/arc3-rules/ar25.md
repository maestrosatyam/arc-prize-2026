## Core rules

- Targets are single cells of colour 11, pieces fixed colour-5 shapes, mirrors full-length colour-10 lines, mirror images colour 4, background 9 [L37-56, L595-694, L1043, L1436; ar25.core.c9; source-only].
- Each piece is shown with its images under repeated reflection in all mirrors (vertical x -> 2mx - x, horizontal y -> 2my - y); two crossing mirrors give up to 4 copies: the piece, its left-right image, its up-down image and its 180-degree image about the crossing [L1416-1424, L1533-1596, L1660-1664; ar25.core.c10; engine].
- Win: every target cell covered by a piece cell or an image cell; covering non-target cells is allowed [L1666-1674; ar25.core.c11; engine].
- The win check runs only after a move action; the next level loads on the following engine step [L1745-1748, L1690-1693; ar25.core.c12; source-only].
- At level start the first movable mirror is selected, else the first piece [L1335-1338; ar25.core.c8; engine].
- The L1 mirror is fixed: it cannot be selected or moved [L645-694, L1336-1337, L1706-1708, L1778, L1792; ar25.core.c7; engine].
- Step budget 64 (L1-L2), 128 (L3-L5), 320 (L6-L8); moves and ACTION5 cost one; reaching 0 gives GAME_OVER [L710-1038, L1084-1093, L1749-1751, L1766-1767; ar25.core.c13; engine].
- HUD: column x=63, one pixel per step, shrinking from the top; segment colours 11, 12, 15, 8, 14 [L1076-1082, L1095-1125; ar25.core.c14; source-only].
- L2 only: if the selection was never changed and fewer than 50 steps remain, a colour-0 marker blinks once over the piece for 8 frames [L1680-1689, L1752-1754; ar25.core.c15; source-only].
- Rotating pieces and single-axis pieces exist in code but no level places them [L1347-1351, L1427, L1590-1592, L1737-1744, L696-1041; ar25.core.c16; source-only].
- No randomness [L1240-1838; ar25.core.c17; source-only].
