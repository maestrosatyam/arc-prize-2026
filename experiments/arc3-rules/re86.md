## Core rules

- Pieces (tag 0031cppcuvqlbi) are single-colour line shapes on background 5: plus-crosses, diagonal X-crosses, diamond or rectangle outlines, a straight line. Exactly one piece is selected; the selected piece shows a black (0) pixel at its sprite centre [L1753-1754, L2075, L2078-2083; re86.core.c2; engine E2].
- Targets: one full-screen target sprite (tag 0054xnsuqceejm) per level draws 3x3 markers, a yellow (4) ring with a coloured centre. The level is won when every non-yellow target pixel is covered by a piece pixel of the same colour in the composite of all pieces (later pieces in level order on top; a selected piece's black centre counts as colour 0) [L1877-1901, L2137-2138; re86.core.c3; engine E3].
- Paint stations (tag 0007dtbisvazhv, from L4): squares with a colour-2 border and a coloured fill. A moved piece overlapping a station of another colour is recoloured to the station colour (spreads over a few frames inside the same action); touching another station later recolours it again [L2061-2074, L2086-2106; re86.core.c5; engine E5].
- Ring obstacles (tag 0003dlchiwseii, colour 1: 8x8 at (28,28) in L6-L7, two 5x5 in L8) deform the piece that touches them [L1936-2042]:
  - Plus-crosses (one full vertical and one full horizontal line; the crossing need not be at the sprite centre): if after the move the vertical line lies in the ring's column span and the horizontal line in its row span, the move is undone; if only the line perpendicular to the move touches the ring, the piece moves but that line stays at its old screen position (it shifts by 3 inside the sprite); if only the line parallel to the move touches, the piece stays and the other line shifts 3 in the move direction. Lines cannot leave the sprite [L1987-2042; re86.core.c6; engine E6].
  - Outlines (tag 0036ilsgwuvbxv): a horizontal push makes the outline a rectangle 3 narrower and 3 taller, a vertical push 3 shorter and 3 wider; the touching edge stays put; width + height is constant; nothing happens when the pushed dimension is 6 or less [L1939-1986; re86.core.c7; engine E7].
- A move is refused if the piece's sprite centre would leave the 64x64 screen [L1926-1934; re86.core.c8; source-only].
- Step budget StepCounter per level: 100, 100, 200, 200, 250, 200, 300, 400 [L1603-1725]. Each action decrements it; at 0 without a win the game is lost (GAME_OVER) [L1816-1820, L2139-2140; re86.core.c4; engine E4].
- HUD: bottom row 63, colour 15 for the remaining budget fraction, colour 1 for the used part [L1826-1839; re86.core.c9; source-only].
- No randomness in the game code [whole file; re86.core.c10; source-only].
