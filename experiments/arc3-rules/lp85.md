## Core rules

- Tracks: each level names a table of one or more tracks; a track is a numbered sequence of cells on a 3-pixel lattice (cell number n at grid (3*col, 3*row)). A track need not be a closed path: the step from its last cell to its first can jump across the board [L1561-21183, L21220-21240; lp85.core.c1; engine E18].
- A button tagged for track T and direction R or L moves every sprite standing exactly on a cell of T one step along T, simultaneously: R moves cell k to k+1 (last to first), L moves k to k-1 (first to last) [L21243-21264, L21377-21410; lp85.core.c1; engine E18].
- Movable pieces: 2x2 decorative tiles (colours 9, 1, 10, 2, 15) and 2x2 goals: colour 11 in all levels and colour 12 in L3 and L4. The 4x4 outlined frames (colour 11, colour 12) and the buttons are off the lattice and never move [L13-1068, L21357-21361; lp85.core.c2; engine E19].
- Buttons: 3x4 arrows; colour 8 = L (backward), colour 14 = R (forward). A click applies every button whose box contains the clicked cell, so buttons drawn at the same place fire together (L4 repeats each button four times; L6, L7, L8 stack buttons of several tracks) [L21376-21388; lp85.core.c3; engine E20].
- Win: after each button click, if every colour-11 frame has a colour-11 goal at its centre (the cell one pixel inside its corner) and every colour-12 frame has a colour-12 goal at its centre, the level is cleared [L21411-21415, L21420-21429; lp85.core.c4; engine E21].
- Budget: per-level click budget (StepCounter) 13, 60, 80, 150, 80, 80, 80, 80. Each button click that does not win uses 1; when the remaining count reaches 0 the game is over. The budget resets at each level start [L1099, L1159, L1205, L1269, L1306, L1430, L1462, L1556, L21279-21289, L21333-21338, L21340-21342, L21416-21418; lp85.core.c5, lp85.core.c6; engine E22, E23].
- Clicks that hit no button change nothing and use no budget [L21373-21396; lp85.core.c7; engine E24].
- HUD: display column 0 fills from the top in colour 5 with the used share of the budget, the rest colour 14; row 1 shows one 4-pixel dash per level starting at x=10, colour 14 for completed levels, colour 5 otherwise [L21290-21315; lp85.core.c8; source-only].
- No randomness; only ACTION6 [L21330; lp85.core.c9; source-only].

## Levels

Track names (A, B, ...) are the table keys; "A_L" = backward button of track A. Click points used in the checks are display pixels.

### L1
- Layout: 32x19 grid; one 20-cell rectangular track A (x6-24, y3-15); colour-11 goal at (21,3) = cell 6; colour-11 frame around cell 1 (6,3); A_L on the left, A_R on the right; budget 13 [L1070-1102, L1562-1572].
- New: everything.
- KEY INSIGHT: a click rotates the whole track by one cell; the goal must be carried into the frame, and only the backward direction (5 clicks) fits the 13-click budget; forward would need 15 [lp85.L1.k1; engine E1, E3].
- Win: A_L x5 (6:5:34 x5) [lp85.L1.k2; engine E1]; the stored human sequence (6 actions, one off-button click) also wins [engine E2].

### L2

- New: several tracks with shared cells.
- KEY INSIGHT: both goals ride loop A but their spacing along A differs from the frames' spacing; a goal standing on a shared cell can be shifted along a row track (C) to change the spacing, after which A carries both into the frames [lp85.L2.k1; engine E4].
- Win: one solution, 8 clicks: A_R, C_R x3, A_R x3, C_R [lp85.L2.k2; engine E4]; human sequence (35 actions with one level reset) [engine E5].

### L5

### L6
- Layout: 60x64; three identical mechanisms (left, right, bottom). Each has three concentric 8-cell square rings (A/B/C, D/E/F, G/H/I) and eight 3-cell spokes (1-8, 9-16, 17-24) that run outward through the three rings; one stacked button turns a mechanism's three rings together, another its eight spokes together. Three 2-cell links (25, 26, 27, stacked on one button) join a cell of each outer ring to one of three colour-11 frames in the centre at (24,27), (30,27), (27,33). One goal per mechanism on its inner ring: (9,18), (45,12), (24,45). All buttons are forward (R); budget 80 [L1310-1433, L5269-21183].
- New: stacked buttons driving many tracks; a final shared link move.
- KEY INSIGHT: spokes move a goal between rings and rings rotate it; each goal has to reach its mechanism's link cell on the outer ring, and since the three links fire on one button, all three goals must be in place before a single final link click [lp85.L6.k1; engine E12].
- Win: one solution, 19 clicks: right spokes x2, bottom spokes x2, left spokes x2, bottom rings x6, left rings x2, right rings x4, links x1 [lp85.L6.k2; engine E12]; human 30 actions [engine E13].
