## Core rules

- Board cells are colour-1 squares on a colour-0 board with colour-5 rim; background colour 10. White pegs are colour-14 discs; grey studs ('p') are fixed pieces; red and blue pegs appear from L6 and L8 [L4620-4632, L4776-4789, L4812, L4880; lf52.core.c1; source-only].
- A selected peg can jump in a direction when the adjacent cell holds a peg or a grey stud and the cell beyond is an empty board cell or a movable platform; the peg lands two cells away [L5331-5345, L5627-5647; lf52.core.c2; engine].
- The jumped piece is removed only if it has the jumper's colour; grey studs and differently coloured pegs stay, so they act as permanent stepping stones [L5369-5388, L5511-5549; lf52.core.c3; engine].
- Win: after a jump, exactly one white peg is left (count of all pegs is 1; L6-L7 count 2 because the red peg stays; L8-L10 the starting number of blue pegs is subtracted) [L5353-5355, L5550-5559, L5622-5624, L5150-5151; lf52.core.c4; engine].
- Movable platforms (',', ';', 'P', '?', 'D', '7') sit on tracks ('-', '|', corners, T-junctions); one arrow press moves every platform whose next cell in that direction has track; platforms carry pegs and studs [L4624-4631, L4741, L4781, L4813, L4845, L4961, L5254-5291; lf52.core.c5; engine].
- Action limit: GAME_OVER once the action count reaches 64 in L1, 320 in L2-L5, 640 in L6-L10 [L5749-5760; lf52.core.c6; engine]. HUD: top row y=0 fills from the left one pixel per action, changing colour each 64 actions (0,1,2,3,4,5) [L5661-5673, L5701-5709; lf52.core.c7; source-only].
- In L1-L3 landing on certain cells (known dead ends) turns the pegs into a frowning face and shows a reset button bottom-left; this is not a loss [L5398-5410, L5585-5620; lf52.core.c9; source-only].
- No randomness in play; an unseeded shuffle only drives the reset-button dissolve animation [L5676-5691, L5722, L5810-5826; lf52.core.c10; source-only].
