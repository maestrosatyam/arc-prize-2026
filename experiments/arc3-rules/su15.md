## Core rules

- Fruits are square blobs whose size encodes a value 0-8: value 0 1x1 colour 10, 1 2x2 colour 6, 2 3x3 colour 15, 3 4x4 colour 11, 4 5x5 colour 12, 5 7x7 colour 8, 6 8x8 colour 9, 7 9x9 colour 7, 8 10x10 colour 14. [sprites '0'-'8', L867; su15.core.c3; source-only]
- Enemies are 4x5 critters in three tiers: tier 1 colour 7, tier 2 colour 14, tier 3 colour 13. [sprites ybnveypak, ybnveypak2, ybnveypak3; L874-876; su15.core.c4; source-only]
- Goal zones are 9x9 colour-9 squares; the header (rows 0-9) shows the required items as icons. [sprites 0008hngjwqibfi and rgjznrcin-tagged icons; L591-801; su15.core.c5; source-only]
- A pulse pulls every fruit or enemy whose box is within 8 px of the click point toward it for 4 frames, up to 4 px per axis per frame, until its centre reaches the point; things farther away are not pulled. [L1113-1170, L1483-1556, L877-879; su15.core.c6; engine]
- After the pulse, pulled fruits that overlap and have the same value merge into one fruit of value+1 at their average centre; two value-8 fruits vanish. [L1202-1257, L1309-1385; su15.core.c7; engine]
- Pulled fruits of different values that end up overlapping cause a flash, a penalty of 2, 4, 6, ... budget units (growing with each offence in the level attempt) and an automatic undo of that click. [L1228-1238, L1435-1452, L1074-1086, L819-823; su15.core.c8; engine]
- Overlapping enemies of the same tier merge into the next tier (two tier-3 enemies vanish); different tiers overlapping are penalised like fruits. [L1259-1307, L1387-1433; su15.core.c9; engine]
- During every pulse frame each enemy that is not being pulled moves 1 px per axis (tier 3: 2 px) toward the nearest fruit; an enemy touching a fruit knocks it away and lowers its value by one (a value-0 fruit is destroyed), then rests for 9 frames. [L1558-1652, L1673-1707, L1709-1809; su15.core.c10; engine]
- Win, checked during and after each pulse: for each requirement the number of fruits of that value (or enemies of that tier) whose centre lies inside any goal zone must equal the required count. [L2011-2059, L1044-1048, L1064-1067; su15.core.c11; engine]
- Requirements: L1 one value-2; L2 one value-3; L3 one value-3 and one value-2; L4 one value-3; L5 one value-3; L6 one value-3 and one tier-1 enemy; L7 two value-3; L8 two value-4 and one tier-2 enemy; L9 one value-4, one tier-3 enemy and one value-2. [L606, L629, L655, L679, L704, L722, L745, L771, L797; su15.core.c12; source-only]
- If the total fruit mass (sum of 2^value) falls below the required mass, the goal zones and header icons turn colour 2; losing all fruits is GAME_OVER; no randomness. [L2121-2150, L1053-1060, L2109-2114, L1068-1069; su15.core.c13; source-only]
- Budget per level 32, 32, 48, 48, 32, 32, 32, 48, 48; each completed pulse costs 1; reaching 0 is GAME_OVER; HUD: bottom row (y=63) colour 0 over the remaining fraction, background colour 5 for the used part; RESET restores the level and budget.
