- Board: floor/path is the colour-5 sprite tagged rsrdfsruqh; cells are 7x7 on a 6 px lattice (x,y = 1+6i). A cell is walkable when its centre pixel lies on the path sprite and not on a visible gate; background 0 [L1823, L2622-2636; g50t.core.c1; engine].

- Goal: 9x9 colour-9 outline (gilbljmfbc). The level is won when the player's cell is exactly inside it (player x,y = goal x+1,y+1) [L2494-2495, L2811-2817; g50t.core.c5; engine].
- Pressure plates (colour-8 hollow square, tag medyellngi) are pressed while any player, ghost or enemy centre lies on them. A plate drives the wire (tag hxztohfdlx, same colour) it sits on; the wire's gate (colour-8 block, tag kjrcloicja) slides 6 px in the direction given by its rotation (0 down, 90 left, 180 up, 270 right) and slides back when the plate empties [L2089-2154, L2176-2199, L2232-2239, L2642-2653, L2723-2727; g50t.core.c6; engine].
- Toggle variant (colour 11): a gate whose centre pixel is colour 11 ignores releases and flips open/closed on every press; its plate and wire are colour 11 too [L2165-2166, L2177-2181; g50t.core.c7; engine].
- Crushing: a gate returning onto the player, or an enemy ending a move on the player, crushes it and the game is lost [L2215-2229, L2463-2469, L2497-2499, L2819-2820; g50t.core.c8; source-only].
- Ghosts: every successful move is appended to a history [L2655-2658]. ACTION5 rewinds everything step by step (plates are re-pressed on the way back), then the current body becomes a ghost (colour 2, centre 5) and a fresh player appears at the start. On every later successful player move k, each ghost performs its own recorded move k if it has one; a ghost whose recording ended stands still, so a ghost that ended on a plate holds it. Failed player moves do not advance k. Ghosts press plates [L2659-2665, L2676-2696, L2730-2755, L2064-2066; g50t.core.c2; engine].
- Ghost slots: one per colour-1 square in the top-left HUD (L1 2, L2-L7 3). Each ACTION5 advances the slot; the ACTION5 that would fill the last slot deletes all ghosts and restarts at slot 0, so L1 allows 1 ghost and later levels 2. A colour-9 bar under the HUD marks the current slot [L1619-1620, L2610-2617, L2714-2722, L2737-2755; g50t.core.c4; engine].
- Teleport pads (colour-15 squares, tag mpreboxmgc) come in pairs joined to a hidden swapper (tag hgglgttaui). A press of a colour-15 plate (not during rewind) makes the swapper exchange the occupants of the two pads after a short animation; a press while nobody is on a pad is wasted [L2242-2345, L2537-2547, L2574-2600; g50t.core.c9; engine].
- Enemies (L6-L7, tag vtwcsmdoqp) step 6 px along a track after each successful player move, keeping direction where possible, and press plates they pass, including colour-11 toggles [L2414-2469, L2666-2674, L2723-2725; g50t.core.c10; engine].
