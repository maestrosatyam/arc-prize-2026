- Lives: 3, drawn as 2x2 colour-8 squares at x 56/59/62, y 61-62. A move made with the bar exhausted costs a life and resets player, key, refills, completed goals and moving changers to the level start; losing the third life is GAME_OVER [L1525-1529, L1821, L1960-1991; ls20.core.c8; engine]
