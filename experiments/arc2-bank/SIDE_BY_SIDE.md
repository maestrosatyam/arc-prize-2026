# Side by side: the earlier evaluation against the bank (2026-10-27)

For the next experiments every decision records both readings before launch and after the board, and neither alone
decides. "Earlier" is what was used until now: a replay on a dev or tune subset of one GPU run (25-65
outputs, with its run noise), the commit run's path checks, and the public board reading. "Bank" is the replay on
the 145 outputs (`scripts/arc2_bank_replay.py`: net, sign test, gates) with the bootstrap spread (`bootstrap.json`)
and, for a submitted build, its row in `calibration_ledger.json`. A disagreement in sign is reported with both
numbers; the period ends when it is closed.
