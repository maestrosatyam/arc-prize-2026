## The earlier issue is in both submissions

- **The ranking issue.** It was found Oct 26, after the submission. When no program passes the
  demonstrations, an unverified program's output can take attempt_2 over the model's own written grid.
- **It is in both readings.** Both ran the same version, so the issue cannot explain the gap between them.
- **v7-vr2 is the fix (f0e7093).**
- **Its cost per draw.** In every replay row, v7-vr2 never lost an output v7-vr got right:

## Reading

- **Size of the drop.** 5.00 points is 6 tasks of 120.
- **The planning noise figure understates the hybrids.** It was 1.8 per reading, 2.6 for a difference, measured on
  identical B0-only copies. The hybrids add the 27B draw:
  - with about 5 tasks flipping per pair of runs, that alone adds about 1.9 points of SD to a difference;
  - so a difference of two hybrid readings has an SD of about 3.2 points.
  - On that figure 5.00 is about 1.6 SD, about 1 in 9. On the old figure it is 1.9 SD, about 1 in 18.
- **34.31 was the outlier.** The pre-reading estimate for v7-vr was about 30 (27-34). 29.31 is inside it, and 34.31
  was at its top.
- **The harness gain is smaller than first read.** Means against v7 (30.97, one reading):
  - v7-vr 31.81 (+0.84);
  - v7-vr2 32.43 (+1.46);
  - first read: +3.34 from v7-vr's 34.31 alone.
- **v7-vr2 is the better ARC-2 build:**
  - it is never worse than v7-vr on any replayed draw;
  - its mean is higher.
- **v7-vr2's tight pair (0.42 apart) is partly luck.** Its replicates churn like v7-vr's.
